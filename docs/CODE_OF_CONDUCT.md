# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the `react-native-alarms` project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

We value contributions from people of all backgrounds, experience levels, identities, and perspectives. Whether you are reporting a bug, suggesting a feature, improving documentation, writing tests, or contributing code, your participation is appreciated.

We pledge to make participation in our project a positive experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful, considerate, and constructive.
- Welcoming newcomers and helping them understand the project.
- Providing feedback that focuses on code, ideas, and technical decisions rather than individuals.
- Accepting constructive criticism and differing technical opinions gracefully.
- Communicating clearly and professionally in issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions.
- Giving appropriate credit to others for their contributions.
- Prioritizing collaboration and the long-term health of the project.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Insulting, derogatory, or demeaning comments.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained hostile behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Threatening, intimidating, or targeting other contributors.
- Repeatedly engaging in unwanted contact or abusive communication.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional open-source community.

## Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of acceptable behavior.

Maintainers may take appropriate and proportionate corrective action in response to behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories, issues, and pull requests.
- Code reviews and technical discussions.
- Project-related communication channels.
- Public spaces when an individual is officially representing the project or its community.

The same expectations apply whether you are a first-time contributor, a regular community member, or a project maintainer.

## Reporting Unacceptable Behavior

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the project maintainers through the repository owner's published contact information or another designated private reporting channel.

When reporting an incident, please include as much relevant information as you feel comfortable sharing, such as:

- A description of the incident.
- Where and when it occurred.
- Links to relevant issues, pull requests, messages, or other evidence.
- Any additional context that may help the maintainers understand the situation.

Please avoid sharing sensitive personal information unnecessarily.

Maintainers will review reports as fairly and promptly as reasonably possible and will respect the privacy of those involved to the extent practical.

## Enforcement

Depending on the severity and circumstances of a violation, maintainers may take one or more of the following actions:

1. **Correction:** A private or public request to stop the inappropriate behavior.
2. **Warning:** A formal warning and clarification of expected conduct.
3. **Temporary restriction:** Temporary restrictions on participation in project discussions or contributions.
4. **Removal:** Removal of contributions or exclusion from project spaces for serious or repeated violations.

Maintainers will consider the nature, severity, and context of each incident when determining an appropriate response.

## Fairness and Accountability

All community members are expected to follow this Code of Conduct, including project maintainers.

Reports should be handled consistently, fairly, and without retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

Knowingly submitting false reports or retaliating against someone for reporting a concern may itself constitute unacceptable behavior.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), a widely adopted standard for open-source communities.

## Commitment

By participating in the `react-native-alarms` project, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct and help maintain a respectful, collaborative, and inclusive environment for everyone.

Thank you for helping make open-source development better for the entire React Native community.
